I am a PhD student at [DTU Electro](https://electro.dtu.dk/), Technical University of Denmark, in the
**Control, Robotics & Embodied AI (CREA)** group, supervised by [Assoc. Prof. Dimitrios Papageorgiou](https://dimpapageorgiou.github.io/)
(see the [research team](https://dimpapageorgiou.github.io/team/)). My PhD project, started in December 2024, is
**Intelligent Control of Ship-Shore Communication System for Remote Situational Awareness**.

My work is on control and estimation for resilient ship–shore communication systems and autonomous maritime applications.
I develop new control and estimation algorithms for time-varying, uncertain, multi-channel environments, and I pair mathematical
analysis with numerical optimization and simulation. My interests include sliding-mode control, adaptive and fault-tolerant systems,
nonlinear estimation, and resource allocation for reliable maritime communications.

Before DTU Electro, I earned an MSc in Electrical Engineering (Automation and Robot Technology) at DTU and an engineering degree in
Robotics and Control Theory at IMT Atlantique, France. In industry, I have worked on motion control and embedded software at JVL
and Caspian Systems, and on autonomous agricultural vehicles at Technology & Strategy.
