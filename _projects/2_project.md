Started as my MSc thesis at DTU, _Self-Tuning Adaptive Sliding Mode Control for Perturbed Systems_, and continued during my PhD.

- Developed a novel barrier-function-based gain adaptation for rapid system commissioning.
- Provided a rigorous Lyapunov stability proof.
- Discretized and implemented the algorithm, and validated it experimentally.

The continuous-time multi-layer barrier adaptive super-twisting controller {% cite vie2025barrier %} has been extended to discrete time
to handle inter-sample blindness {% cite vie2026discrete %}.
