**PhD project, DTU Electro (December 2024 – present)**
Supervisor: [Assoc. Prof. Dimitrios Papageorgiou](https://dimpapageorgiou.github.io/), Control, Robotics & Embodied AI (CREA).

As vessels become more autonomous, operators ashore need a reliable, real-time picture of what is happening on board and around the ship.
This project develops control and estimation methods for ship–shore communication links that are time-varying, uncertain and multi-channel.
The aim is to keep remote situational awareness reliable even when link quality changes quickly.

Work so far includes a time-varying fading channel model for the maritime environment {% cite vie2025fading %}.
