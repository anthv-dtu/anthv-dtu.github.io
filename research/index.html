Research

My research focuses on control and estimation for resilient ship–shore communication systems and autonomous maritime applications. I develop sliding-mode, adaptive and fault-tolerant control and nonlinear estimation methods for time-varying, uncertain, multi-channel environments, so that remote situational awareness stays reliable.

Research Areas

Safe Mapping Inversion

Control and estimation through safe inversion of nonlinear mappings, with particular emphasis on singularities, constraints, and topology.

Sliding Mode Control and Estimation

Sliding-mode methods for robust control and state estimation in uncertain nonlinear systems.

Diagnosis & Fault-tolerant Control

Model-based methods for fault detection, diagnosis, and condition monitoring of complex dynamical systems.

Nonlinear and Adaptive Control

Nonlinear and adaptive control methods for systems with uncertainty, changing dynamics, and demanding performance requirements.

Maritime Remote & Autonomous Systems

Control, estimation, and decision-making methods for autonomous and remotely operated maritime systems.
